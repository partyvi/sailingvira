These are the places I keep going back to while rebuilding Vira. Nobody paid me to list them; each one is here because it helped me, and the story where I used it is linked next to it.

## The Irwin Citation 34

- [Practical Sailor review](https://www.practical-sailor.com/sailboat-reviews/irwin-citation-34): an honest used-boat review of the Irwin Citation 34.
- [sailboat.guide](https://sailboat.guide/rprl8j): the Irwin Citation 34 specs in one place.
- [Mauri Pro rig data](https://www.mauripro.com/collections/sailboat-data-irwin-34-citation): rig dimensions for the Irwin 34, useful when you order sails or rigging. My own measurements are in [Mast](../mast/).
- [My refit plan on Trello](https://trello.com/b/OBUW2PNY/irwin-34-refit): the to-do board I used to keep the whole refit in order.

## Navigation and boat electronics

- [OpenPlotter](https://openmarine.net/openplotter): the Raspberry Pi system that runs my navigation. See [Navigation instruments & autopilot](../navigation/).
- [OpenCPN](https://opencpn.org): free chart plotter software.
- [Signal K](https://signalk.org): the open data standard that ties all my instruments together.
- [SensESP](https://github.com/SignalK/SensESP): ESP32 sensors that talk to Signal K.
- [PyPilot](https://pypilot.org): the open-source autopilot behind my smart Autohelm drive.
- [OpenMarine forum](https://forum.openmarine.net): the place to ask about OpenPlotter and PyPilot.

## Parts I used

- [Mr. Cool Marine 2QM20 heat exchanger](https://www.mrcool.us/2qm20-yanmar-heat-exchanger.html): the only non-original part I didn't make myself in [Fresh water cooling](../fresh-water-cooling/).
- [Ellebogen GT-SDD55 engine mounts](https://ellebogen.com/us/ellebogen-gt-sdd55/): the new mounts for the Yanmar 2QM20. See [Engine](../engine/).
- [Blue Sea Systems A-Series ELCI, 30 A](https://www.bluesea.com/products/3106100/A-Series_ELCI_Main_Circuit_Breaker_-_Double_Pole_120V_AC_30A): the proper ELCI on my shore power. See [Electrical system](../electrical/).
- [Battery Cables USA](https://www.batterycablesusa.com): made-to-measure marine battery cables with very good crimps, in three days.
- [Colligo Marine technical material](https://www.colligomarine.com/tech-material): a lot of good reading on Dyneema rigging. See [Standing rigging & chainplates](../standing/).
- [SRE Gear AmSteel](https://sregear.com/products/1-4-gray-amsteel-600-spool): 1/4-inch Dyneema I used for the lifelines.
- [Nyalic](https://www.nyalic.com): the clear coating on my mast. See [Mast](../mast/).
- [go2marine stuffing box hoses](https://www.go2marine.com/Stuffing-Box-Hoses?quantity=1&inside-diameter=20): hard-to-find hose sizes.

## Epoxy, coatings and materials

- [The Epoxy Experts MAX GRE](https://theepoxyexperts.com/shop/adhesives/chemical-and-water-resistant/max-gre/max-gre-96oz-epoxy-resin-gasoline-diesel-fuel-resistant-epoxy-coating-adhesive/): the fuel-resistant epoxy for my [Fuel tank](../fuel/).
- [Jotun Tankguard DW](https://www.jotun.com/us-en/industries/products/Tankguard-DW.): a drinking-water tank coating I looked at for the [water tanks](../water/).
- [Epoxy USA](http://www.epoxyusa.com/): epoxy supplies.
- [Wolverine LiquaTile](http://www.wolverinecoatings.com/TDS/LiquaTile_1172-TDS.pdf): the data sheet of a coating I used.
- [TAP Plastics foamed PVC sheets](https://www.tapplastics.com/product/plastics/cut_to_size_plastic/foamed_pvc_sheets/342): the 6 mm foamed PVC for my headliner and back panels. See [Interior woodwork](../interior-wood/).
- [Xometry metal supplies](https://supplies.xometry.com/6063-aluminum-bar-rectangleom-18084-12-0.html): the 6063 aluminum bar for my mast steps.

## Making parts

- [SendCutSend](https://sendcutsend.com): laser-cut metal parts, like my [radar mount](../radar-mount/) and the alternator tensioner bar.
- [JLCPCB](https://jlcpcb.com): circuit boards for my [small electronics projects](../small-electronics/).
- [JLC3DP](https://jlc3dp.com): 3D printing in metal; my stainless AIS clamps were printed there.

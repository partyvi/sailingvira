I'm Viktor. I'm an engineer, and I like to know how things work, so at some point I stopped buying little boxes for the boat and started designing my own.

## How I got here

I sailed on Lake Travis almost every weekend for more than five years before I took any formal course. My first boat was Sea Sun, a Hunter 19. In November 2021 I found an Irwin Citation 34 on Facebook Marketplace for $5,000, half an hour from home. I wasn't looking for a project boat. You can guess how that went.

Since then I've rebuilt Vira almost piece by piece: the deck, the interior, the wiring, the engine, the rig. I kept a diary of every work day, and this site turns that diary into stories, one per project.

## Who sails with me

My wife Maria, who survives my "gentle sail" forecasts. My sons, who carried an engine up 140 steps with me. My father, who helped from the first days. And friends who come along when the wind is right.

## The workshop

At home I have a CNC, 3D printers and a soldering iron, and most of the parts on this site started on a notebook page. The [Workshop](../workshop/) has the files, free for your own boat.

## Where Vira is now

In 2026 Vira left Lake Travis for Kemah, on Galveston Bay. Big water, at last. The [Sailing](../voyages/) pages tell how she got there, and the [Boat diary](https://irwin34.blogspot.com) keeps going day by day.
